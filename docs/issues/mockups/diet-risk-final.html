Scoring rules
  • · High-histamine — Σ histamine_score across all confirmed ingredients (a cumulative load, not a count). Flag triggers when any one ingredient has score ≥ 2.
  • · High-FODMAP — count of ingredients where any subcategory (oligos / fructose / polyols / lactose) is high.
  • · Gluten — count of ingredients with contains_gluten = true.
  • · Dairy — count of ingredients with contains_dairy = true.
