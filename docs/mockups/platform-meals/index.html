Platform meals — UX prototypes

Log curated dishes (e.g. Arroz de pato) composed from the ingredient catalog, with default icons instead of photos — for restaurants, shared meals, or anytime a camera is awkward.

Matches Food & drinks card Interactive · mobile frame Pick one direction
Option A

Meal library browser

Third capture action: From library. Browse by cuisine, tap an icon tile, set meal time, done. Discovery-first — best when the platform catalog is the main path.

Option B

Unified quick-log search

Extend Log again into one search across your history and the platform library. Type “arroz” → hit the catalog dish. Smallest mental-model change.

Option C

Build from ingredients

Log without photo → name the dish → assemble ingredients from your catalog (or start from a platform template). Icons + diet flags fall out of the ingredient list. Best for dishes not yet curated.

Scenario to try in each mockup: you ate Arroz de pato at a restaurant, didn’t take a photo, and it’s not in your past meals.
