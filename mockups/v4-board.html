Daily Board — V2 cards × V3 timeline

Take V2's rich, scannable cards and lay them out chronologically along V3's timeline. Each event in the day is a card. Time‑grouped sections (Morning · Midday · Evening · End of day) give the day shape without losing the "log throughout the day, or fill at the end" flexibility. The aggregate insights rail and hero stats strip stay.

From V2

Rich, scannable cards

Each event is a self‑contained V2‑style card with its own focus mode on mobile — same big photos, same ingredient‑grid editor, same hero stats strip.

From V3

Chronological spine

Time‑grouped sections (Morning · Midday · Evening · End of day) with hour dots, sticky section headers, and a left rail for jumping.

New in V4

End‑of‑day rollup card

A pinned card at the bottom of the timeline captures the wellbeing scales (bloating, stool, joint, neuro). Log live or fill once at night — both work.

Cohesion

One day, three lenses

Left rail = navigate · center = log · right = patterns. The same data shown three ways, like Linear's issue ↔ board ↔ roadmap.
