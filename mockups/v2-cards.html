Cards Canvas — modular dashboard

Drop the giant scrolling form. Each section (food, gut, mind, sleep, supplements, notes) becomes a self‑contained card. Desktop lays them out in a 3‑column masonry; mobile stacks them and each card has a “focus mode” that opens a clean full‑screen editor — no more cramped inputs.

V1 Workspace V2 Cards V3 Timeline
Desktop wins

Density without overload

Masonry cards let users see the whole day in one glance. Bigger photos. Mini‑charts that compare to the last 7 days.

Mobile wins

Focus mode = one task, one screen

Tap a card → full‑screen editor with comfortable inputs. The ingredient editor becomes its own page, not a 6pt inline row.

Personal feel

Reorder cards to match how you log

Drag the card grid. Photos first if that's how your day flows; symptoms first if you triage gut issues. The board adapts to you.
