Workspace — split‑pane productivity

Treat the app like a Notion/Linear‑style productivity tool. Desktop earns its screen with a 3‑pane layout (nav · canvas · inspector). Mobile stays focused single‑column, but heavy editing tasks open in roomy bottom sheets instead of cramping themselves inline.
