      Cost assumptions (saved in this browser)

      You buy on buycycle and resell on buycycle at the comparables median minus the haircut.
      Total cost = price × (1 + buyer protection %) + fixed € + payment/FX % × price + inbound shipping + packaging + repair + buffer.
      Net sale = expected sale × (1 − seller fee %) − outbound shipping − boost/relist. Profit = net sale − total cost; ROI = profit ÷ total cost.
