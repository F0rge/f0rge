# Firstout

The back office of a Gauteng furniture retailer: stock, the till and the books. It exists so that a sale is priced, taxed and recorded the same way whichever document carries it. Decisions: [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/).

## Language

### Pricing

**Sale document**:
A till sale, quote, sales order, layby, tax invoice or storefront order: any document that charges a customer for SKUs.
_Avoid_: Transaction, docket, books document

**Sale line pricing**:
The one rule that turns a SKU, quantity, customer and Line discount into a line's unit price, amount and VAT, whichever Sale document holds the line.
_Avoid_: Till pricing, reprice, discounted unit

**Price list**:
A hand-made set of unit prices for chosen SKUs, assigned to a customer; where it names a SKU it wins over the customer's Classification tier. Lists generated by the Cin7 import are not Price lists.
_Avoid_: Cin7 tier list, customer pricing

**Classification tier**:
The price band a customer buys at: Stockist, Wholesale, Decorator or Retail. Each SKU carries one price per tier.
_Avoid_: Classification, price tier, price classification, customer type, trade/retail

**Retail price**:
A SKU's price in the Retail tier: what a walk-in pays, and the fallback when a customer's tier has no price for that SKU.
_Avoid_: List price, selling price, shelf price

**Line discount**:
A percentage taken off one line's amount on a Sale document.
_Avoid_: Discounted unit, markdown

**Price override**:
Staff setting a line's unit price to something other than the one Sale line pricing resolved; a discount by another name.
_Avoid_: Manual price, custom price, typed price

**Discount cap**:
The team's highest Line discount percentage that staff may give without manager authority.
_Avoid_: Max till discount, discount limit

### VAT

**VAT rate snapshot**:
The VAT rate a Sale document was priced with, kept on the document so reprints, credit notes and conversions use it rather than today's rate.
_Avoid_: VAT multiplier, current rate

**Time of supply**:
The moment SARS treats a supply as made, which fixes its VAT rate: generally the earlier of invoice or payment. A layby fixes its rate when it is concluded.
_Avoid_: Sale date, invoice date

**Tax invoice issue**:
The moment a tax invoice gets its number and issue date; from then on its lines and amounts change only through a credit note.
_Avoid_: Posting, finalising, proforma

### Stock

**Hold cost**:
The unit cost of stock a sales order took off its location's shelf to hold for the customer, kept so that returning or delivering that stock uses the same cost.
_Avoid_: Average cost, landed cost, held sale
