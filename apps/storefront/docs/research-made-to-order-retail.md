# Made-to-order furniture: how established retailers run it

Research for DasKasas adding supplier-made ("made-to-order") listings alongside floor stock. Gathered 2026-11-21 from retailers' own pages. Every fact has a URL. Where a site blocked automated reading, the fact comes from the search engine's copy of that retailer's own page and is marked *(snippet)*. Those are less reliable and should be checked by hand before anyone quotes them.

## Summary

- **Deposit:** from 10% to 100%. DFS takes 10% on made-to-order upholstery. Sophie Conran takes 50% (25% if the fabric isn't chosen yet). Mobelli accepts a minimum 50%, and it is non-refundable. Weylandts, Zorora and Coricraft (per its FAQ) take 100% before production. SA retailers lean to 100% up front. The UK ones split the payment.
- **Balance:** due before the goods leave, never on the doorstep. DFS: 3 days before the booked delivery. Sophie Conran: within 7 days of the "ready" notice, and nothing is released unpaid. Weylandts and Block & Chisel: no collection or delivery until paid in full and EFT has cleared.
- **Lead time:** 2–12 weeks, mostly 4–8. Benchmade Modern: 2–5 weeks of production. Zorora: 10–20 business days plus 3–7 days delivery. Coricraft: 4–6 or 4–8 weeks. MRP Home: 6–8 weeks. DFS: 4–12 weeks. The best ones say when the clock starts: Coricraft "from receipt of full payment", sofa.com "from order acceptance" *(snippet)*, Zorora "from order confirmation".
- **Cancellation:** a short free window, then a fee or lost deposit. Free windows: 24 hours (Joybird, Benchmade, Zorora), 5 working days (Sophie Conran) or 7 days (Coricraft FAQ, sofa.com *(snippet)*). After that: 10% (Coricraft FAQ, sofa.com, Benchmade), deposit forfeited (Mobelli 50%, Zorora 100%), 75% of retail (Weylandts) or no cancellation at all (Joybird made-to-order, Sophie Conran, Coricraft T&C). Change-of-mind returns of made-to-order goods: almost universally excluded. Article and Benchmade are the exceptions, with paid returns.
- **Supplier risk** shows up as four clauses: a long delay lets the customer choose to wait, reselect or take a refund (Coricraft); the price is locked at confirmation (Loaf, Joybird) or explicitly not locked (Mobelli adjusts for the exchange rate); materials on backorder show a later date at checkout (Benchmade); and storage fees apply once the piece is ready and not taken (Coricraft, Weylandts, Mobelli, Sophie Conran).
- **Retailers contradict themselves.** Coricraft's FAQ says full payment and free cancellation within 7 days. Its T&C say a "required deposit" and "cannot cancel". sofa.com's help centre and its terms disagree the same way *(snippet)*. One source of truth matters.
- **SA law note (not legal advice).** CPA s17 lets a consumer cancel an advance order subject to a "reasonable" deposit and cancellation charge. s17(1) excludes **special-order goods**, which is what made-to-order is. [acts.co.za CPA s17](https://www.acts.co.za/consumer-protection/17_consumer_s_right_to_cancel_advance_reservation_booking_or_order). Get the T&C wording checked by a lawyer.

## Per retailer

### Coricraft (SA, own factory in Cape Town)
- Made-to-order page: four steps (Silhouette, Material, Ordering & Lead Time, Shipping & Delivery). Says "Lead times typically range between 4 and 6 weeks". Advises ordering material swatches because dye lots and screens vary. Contacts the customer when the piece reaches the warehouse. https://www.coricraft.co.za/pages/made-to-order
- FAQ: 4–8 weeks "depending on our factory's capacity and availability of any raw materials". Fabric or leather can be changed, dimensions and design can't. Sent to manufacture only after "full payment was received". Changes and cancellation free within 7 working days, then a 10% handling fee. An SMS goes out with the delivery date and slot, and a second at dispatch with tracking. https://coricraft.co.za/pages/faqs
- T&C: 4.4 "Once you have paid the required deposit, we begin manufacturing"; customised goods can't be changed, cancelled or refunded. 5.7 extended lead times are "noted on our website at the time of purchase", and a delay of a month or more gives the choice to wait, reselect or take a refund. 5.12 storage is 10% of order value per month if delivery isn't confirmed within 30 days of completion, and the order can be cancelled after 3 months. 6.1 made-to-order goods can't be refunded or exchanged. https://www.coricraft.co.za/pages/terms-and-conditions
- Listings *(snippet, pages rate-limited)*: delivery-time filter buckets (1–2 / 9–10 / >10 weeks) on the leather couch category. Bianca couch: "21 Colours Available" and "6 weeks from receipt of full payment". Santorini slipcover: lead time differs by fabric (9–10 weeks). https://coricraft.co.za/collections/leather-couch, https://www.coricraft.co.za/bianca-fully-upholstered-corner-couch, https://coricraft.co.za/products/santorini-slipcover-corner-couch_464

### Weylandts (SA)
- Made-to-order is handmade in the Cape Town warehouse. Fabrics and finishes are fixed per range (named leathers, fabrics, oak finishes). To find out which options apply, the customer checks the product or uses the enquiry form. https://www.weylandts.co.za/made-to-order
- The online product page shows "In Stock – Available" and delivery of 4–14 working days. No made-to-order flow online. https://www.weylandts.co.za/anderson-modular-sofa
- T&C §5: special orders need 100% up front. Lead time "depends on the goods, their availability" and supplier performance, and is shown on the sales order. Delays are notified. The made-to-order cancellation fee is "75% of the retail price". §11 excludes custom goods from returns. §6 collection within 10 days of the ready notice, then R250 a day storage. https://www.weylandts.co.za/terms-and-conditions

### Mobelli (SA)
- FAQ: the "advance order" tag means "items will be made specifically for you and that a waiting period will apply". Wicker, frame colour and fabric can be changed. Lead time comes from the account manager. Personalised or specially made items can't be returned. https://mobelli.co.za/frequently-asked-questions/
- T&C 3.5: 100% secures the quoted price. 50% "(no less)" is accepted and is non-refundable. The order total stays "subject to change based on exchange rate pricing fluctuations when stock arrives". §4: 5% storage after 3 months. https://mobelli.co.za/terms-and-conditions/

### Zorora Sofas (SA, online-first)
- FAQ: 10–20 business days of manufacture, and the lead time is shown above Add to Cart. Three fabric collections. Up to 4 free swatches. 100% deposit to start production. Non-refundable if cancelled more than 24 hours after ordering. Change-of-mind returns only with approval, less 10% and the delivery fee. Delivery can be pushed later but not earlier. https://zororasofas.co.za/pages/faqs
- Blog: "3 and 5 weeks from order confirmation to delivery". Premium fabrics "may require additional sourcing time". At the time of reading, a site banner said made-to-order orders placed now dispatch from January 2027, which shows how a seasonal backlog gets communicated. https://zororasofas.co.za/blogs/news/how-long-custom-sofa-delivery-south-africa

- *(snippet)* "Made To Order" in product names and a dedicated category. Production starts after the order is placed and paid. About 6–8 weeks for manufacture and delivery. One listing says no refunds, returns or cancellations. https://www.mrphome.com/shop/furniture/shop-made-to-order, https://www.mrphome.com/en_za/georgia-3-seater-sofa-made-to-order-106165433

### Block & Chisel (SA)
- Refund policy: "Made-to-order, custom or special-order items" are not returnable unless defective. Defects within 6 months: repair, replace or refund. https://blockandchisel.co.za/policies/refund-policy
- *(snippet, pages 404 to the fetcher)*: goods are not dispatched until paid in full and EFT has cleared. https://www.blockandchisel.co.za/node/35

### DFS (UK): help centre returns 403 to the fetcher; all *(snippet)*
- 10% deposit with the balance 3 days before the booked delivery, made-to-order upholstery only. Other ranges are paid in full. https://help.dfs.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/4408290099729-How-to-pay-online-for-items-that-are-due-for-delivery
- 4–12 weeks, with 7- and 21-day express ranges. https://help.dfs.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/4408183388689-I-need-my-furniture-in-a-hurry-can-you-help
- "Track My Order" shows the estimated timeframe while the piece is built. DFS calls 3–4 days before delivery to book a slot. https://help.dfs.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/46168738112401-How-do-I-stay-up-to-date-with-my-order

### sofa.com (UK): 403; all *(snippet)*
- Free swatches, up to 6 per order, and showroom swatches to take home. https://www.sofa.com/free-swatches
- Terms: 4–8 weeks from order acceptance. Customised orders can't be cancelled. The help centre says a 10% fee after 7 days. https://www.sofa.com/gb/terms, https://help.sofa.com/support/home

### Loaf (UK): 403; all *(snippet)*
- Amendments within 14 days. A change of product, fabric or colour means cancel and re-order at the current price. No cancellation once delivery is arranged. https://loaf.com/helphub/returns
- 10.3: price changes don't affect orders already confirmed. https://loaf.com/legal/terms-and-conditions
- Brioche sofa: 34 shades across 6 fabrics, free swatches. https://loaf.com/products/brioche-sofa

### Sophie Conran (UK)
- 50% at confirmation, 25% when production starts, 25% when ready. With the fabric undecided, 25% locks the price and production waits for 50%. Balance within 7 days of the "ready" notice: "We do not release any piece for delivery until it is paid in full." Free cancellation for 5 working days, after which none. Fabric bought for the order is non-refundable. Updates: confirmation, production start, then photos of the finished piece and a delivery booking. 4 weeks of free storage, then £50 per item per week. If the balance is still unpaid 60 days after it falls due, the order may be treated as cancelled and the payments kept. https://www.sophieconran.com/pages/payment-terms-made-to-order-furniture

### Joybird (US)
- Made-to-order: free cancellation within 24 hours, no cancellation after that, no returns. Ready-to-ship: 15% fee. https://joybird.com/returns-and-exchanges
- "Buy Now, Decide Later": locks the price, comes with a free swatch kit and gives 90 days to choose. Production starts when the fabric is chosen. No choice after 90 days means cancellation with a 10% fee. https://joybird.com/decide-later
- Build updates "from placement through arrival". The customer can delay production. 30 days to schedule once ready, or the order is treated as abandoned. https://joybird.com/shipping-and-delivery

### Benchmade Modern (US)
- 2–5 weeks of production plus 7–15 business days shipping. Backordered materials show an estimated date at checkout. Free within 24 hours, then a 10% fee. Free swatch kits. "Decide Fabric Later", with fabric upcharges invoiced later. https://benchmademodern.com/faq
- The team emails if the estimated production or delivery date changes. https://benchmademodern.com/pages/benchmade-modern-lead-times

## Patterns worth copying for a small showroom retailer

1. **Make "Made to order" visible before the click.** Show a badge on the card plus a filter. Coricraft filters by delivery-time bucket; Mobelli uses an "advance order" tag; MRP Home puts it in the product name and a separate category. Put the lead time next to the buy button, as Zorora does.
2. **State the lead time as a range from a named event,** for example "6 weeks from receipt of full payment" (Coricraft) or "from order confirmation" (Zorora). For DasKasas the event is the order being registered in the back office: show the customer the resulting date, not only "4–8 weeks".
3. **Deposit to start, balance before release.** The UK pattern is a deposit up front, with the balance due a few days before delivery or within 7 days of the "ready" notice, and nothing released unpaid (DFS, Sophie Conran). 50% is the common middle (Mobelli, Sophie Conran). Set it so the deposit at least covers what DasKasas must pay its supplier up front.
4. **A short free-cancellation window, then a stated fee.** Use 24 hours to 7 days, then a fixed percentage or the deposit is lost (Joybird, Zorora, Coricraft FAQ, sofa.com). Write it once, on the product page and in the T&C, word for word. Coricraft and sofa.com contradict themselves between FAQ and terms.
5. **Milestone updates, not a live countdown.** Use confirmed → in production → ready (with a photo) → delivery booked (Sophie Conran; DFS "Track My Order"; Coricraft SMS). Proactively email when the date moves (Benchmade, Weylandts §5).
6. **Delay clause with customer options.** If the date slips by a month or more, the customer chooses to wait, reselect or take a refund (Coricraft 5.7). Showing a seasonal backlog on the site (Zorora's January banner) avoids disputes.
7. **Lock the price at confirmation.** Loaf 10.3 and Joybird lock it. Mobelli's exchange-rate adjustment pushes supplier risk onto the customer. DasKasas should absorb supplier price changes after confirmation, or say clearly that it won't.
8. **Swatches: a limited free number plus the showroom.** Zorora allows 4, sofa.com up to 6 plus showroom take-home. Add a dye-lot / screen-colour disclaimer (Coricraft). A small showroom can lend physical swatches instead of posting them.
9. **A collection deadline once the piece is ready.** 10–30 days free, then a storage fee (Weylandts, Coricraft, Sophie Conran). This stops finished pieces clogging a small floor.
