Proposed rule: archiving hides a SKU from new sales, purchasing, reorders and kit selection. It preserves stock, photos, prices, barcodes, kit recipes and all past records. Existing commitments, stocktakes and returns can still be completed. Archived stock stays visible in stock screens and reports; physical stock corrections remain a separate action.
